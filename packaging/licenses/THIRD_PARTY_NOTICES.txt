Audio Assembler - third-party software
=====================================

Audio Assembler itself is released under the MIT License (Audio-Assembler-MIT.txt).
The Windows application also contains the following separate open-source components.
Each keeps its own license. Full license texts are in this folder.


1. FFmpeg 9.0.2 with the LAME 3.100 MP3 encoder (audio engine)
   File:     _internal\ffmpeg\ffmpeg.exe
   License:  FFmpeg: GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later (FFmpeg\LGPL-2.1.txt,
             FFmpeg\FFmpeg-LICENSE.md). LAME: GNU Library General Public License 2 or later
             (FFmpeg\LAME-COPYING.txt). This build contains no GPL or non-free parts.
   Source:   https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
             https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/lame/lame/3.100/lame-3.100.tar.gz
             Copies of both source archives, and the build script used
             (packaging/ffmpeg/build_ffmpeg.sh), are published with every Audio Assembler release
             at https://github.com/formful-dev/audio-assembler/releases
   Build settings: FFmpeg\BUILDINFO.txt
   Audio Assembler runs ffmpeg.exe as a separate program. You may replace it with your own build.
